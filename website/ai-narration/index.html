THE WORKFLOW

Make the most of your voice studio.

01

Section-based editing

Break a long text into sections so you can adjust one part without rearranging the entire manuscript. Reorder sections to revise the story's flow.

02

Multiple narrators

Assign available voice profiles to individual sections for dialogue or changes in narrator. Generate and review passages to assess consistency between sections.

03

Sound effects and timing

Where supported, add sound effects per section, adjust timing and control whether effects end or continue across section boundaries.

04

Export finished audio

Desktop production workflows support generating audio files using available export formats. Check each model and platform for current capabilities and generation performance.
