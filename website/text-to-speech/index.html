THE WORKFLOW

Make the most of your voice studio.

01

Start with a script

Paste written text into Narration Studio and break it into manageable sections. Keeping sections separate helps you check pacing and pronunciation without treating an entire project as one block.

02

Choose voices across sections

Assign available voices to individual sections to distinguish characters, speakers or narration styles. Voice availability and features depend on the installed engines.

03

Manage pacing and sound

Use supported controls for section speed and delays, and arrange sound effects alongside narration. Review individual sections and export your completed audio using available output formats.

04

Understand local requirements

Humperdinck desktop uses local speech models after downloading the models required by your chosen engine. The browser experience is more limited, and output speed varies by hardware and model.
