01

Who operates Humperdinck

Humperdinck is a product operated by an individual sole proprietor based in Texas, United States. The operator's legal name and a monitored contact channel must be confirmed before public launch. See the contact section below.

02

Information you choose to provide

When using local desktop features, you may enter text, select voices, upload recordings, and generate audio. Those materials are intended to be processed locally by the selected installed engine. Some features download model files or check for updates online. Do not assume all engines, plugins, or optional integrations operate offline; check their notices before enabling them.

03

Website and Studio

Our public pages may receive standard technical request information from our hosting provider, including IP addresses and network/security logs. The browser Studio may retrieve software or model files from Cloudflare and third-party hosting services; those providers process connection data according to their own policies. If you submit information through a feature that is not yet enabled, it is not necessarily stored.

04

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and page performance. Cloudflare describes this tool as cookieless and not designed to fingerprint visitors. We may separately count page views and installer-link clicks by platform using anonymous event categories. These are aggregate measurements, not user profiles. Analytics availability depends on deployment settings.

05

Storage, sharing, and security

Locally saved projects, recordings and generated audio ordinarily remain under your control on your device unless you intentionally export or share them. Security, backups, deletion and retention on that device are your responsibility. Online services, downloads and the planned account and purchase features may involve third parties, whose applicable terms and retention periods can differ.

06

Future accounts, payments and community voices

A Humperdinck account will be required to purchase and initially activate a Pro license. Planned sign-in methods are passwordless email verification codes or magic links, plus Google and Apple sign-in. Humperdinck does not intend to require a separate Humperdinck account password. Email authentication will require sending a time-limited verification message, and Google or Apple sign-in may involve sharing account identifiers and selected profile information with the chosen identity service. Account registration and checkout have not yet launched on the audited public website. Once enabled, account and purchase services will necessarily process some information to identify an account, process payment through a provider and administer up to 10 device activations; the exact fields, authentication provider, email delivery provider, payment provider, security controls, retention periods, account deletion rules and privacy choices remain to be established and disclosed before launch. Sign-in method selection must not be represented as implemented until the production authentication flow is tested. After initial activation, the local Pro application is intended to remain functional offline without periodic online license checks. A future community voice library is separate and must receive additional privacy disclosures before uploads open.

07

Your choices and requests

You can control files saved on your device and browser privacy settings. For privacy-related requests involving data held by Humperdinck, use the operator contact method once published. Applicable laws may provide additional rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain data.

08

Children and changes

Humperdinck is not directed to children under 13. Do not upload children's voice recordings without the appropriate rights and consents. We will update the effective date when this policy materially changes.

09

Contact information — publication required
