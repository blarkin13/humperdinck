Pre-launch information. This page explains current practices and planned policies. Certain commercial and account features are not yet available; remaining legal and operational details will be published before those features launch.
01

Who operates Humperdinck

Humperdinck is operated by Brian Larkin, a sole proprietor based in Texas, United States. For customer assistance contact support@humperdinck.studio; for privacy inquiries contact privacy@humperdinck.studio.

02

Information you choose to provide

When using local desktop features, you may enter text, select voices, upload recordings, and generate audio. Those materials are intended to be processed locally by the selected installed engine. Some features download model files or check for updates online. Required account registration, purchase and initial license-activation traffic are separate from optional desktop diagnostics, and do not require diagnostic consent. Do not assume all engines, plugins, or optional integrations operate offline; check their notices before enabling them.

03

Website and Studio

Our public pages may receive standard technical request information from our hosting provider, including IP addresses and network/security logs. The browser Studio may retrieve software or model files from Cloudflare and third-party hosting services; those providers process connection data according to their own policies. If you submit information through a feature that is not yet enabled, it is not necessarily stored.

Optional beta feedback

When the optional beta feedback form is enabled, signed-in adult testers may voluntarily send a summary, troubleshooting details, operating system, feature category and up to three screenshots. This material is stored in restricted Cloudflare D1 and private R2 resources and is accessible to the submitting account and authorized Humperdinck reviewers. Please remove personal information, private scripts, account details and third-party voice content before submitting. Report text and screenshots are scheduled to be removed after 90 days, subject to separate backup retention and any lawful preservation requirements. Testers can remove their own feedback and attached screenshots earlier through the beta portal. A report is not automatically published to GitHub or shared with other testers. Beta submissions are disabled until this feature and its deletion/consent controls are verified.

Invitation-only temporary beta Pro

The beta owner may record an invited email address before the invitee creates an account. Unclaimed invitations expire after 14 days, are not public, and may be canceled by the owner. Invitation creation prepares text that the owner can send manually; the website does not send invitation emails automatically. Upon signing in with the matching Google identity, the invitee can claim 30 days of Pro access. Unclaimed expired invitations and minimal accepted invitation records are subject to the beta invitation retention procedure. Approved testers who sign in with Google may receive 30 days of temporary Pro access without a payment. We record the account identifier, randomized grant identifier, issue and expiration dates, and any revocation date. The desktop receives a signed, installation-bound beta entitlement that expires automatically. Temporary beta access is not a lifetime purchase and cannot be exported as a perpetual license. Revoking an online invitation prevents further issuance but may not invalidate a previously issued offline certificate until its signed expiry. Google account sign-in and the provider's processing of account data are described above.

04

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and page performance. Cloudflare describes this tool as cookieless and not designed to fingerprint visitors. We may separately count page views and installer-link clicks by platform using anonymous event categories. These are aggregate measurements, not user profiles. Analytics availability depends on deployment settings. Desktop diagnostic reports are separate from website analytics. Humperdinck intends to offer optional diagnostics that are disabled by default and transmitted only after a user explicitly opts in. With consent, diagnostic reports may contain crash details, application and model errors, operating-system information and basic hardware or performance characteristics. The app must not automatically transmit recordings, scripts, generated audio or other voice-project content as diagnostics. Technical reports cannot be guaranteed anonymous merely because names are omitted; error messages, file paths or hardware details may sometimes identify a person, so reports should be minimized and scrubbed before transmission. Users must be able to withdraw consent for future diagnostic uploads.

05

Storage, sharing, and security

Locally saved projects, recordings and generated audio ordinarily remain under your control on your device unless you intentionally export or share them. The desktop app also retains a signed-name voice consent record for personal voice imports. For third-party voices, a supplied speaker-signed PDF or image is stored in a private local consent-evidence folder with a SHA-256 checksum in the voice metadata. These documents are not automatically sent to Humperdinck's account or website service, and deleting the related voice deletes its locally linked consent-evidence file. You remain responsible for secure backups and any separately retained original signed agreements. Humperdinck does not provide a community library for publishing or distributing user-created cloned voices. Security, backups, deletion and retention on that device are your responsibility. Online services, downloads and the planned account and purchase features may involve third parties, whose applicable terms and retention periods can differ.

Customer correspondence. Messages sent to support@, billing@, privacy@ or legal@humperdinck.studio are routed through Cloudflare Email Routing, forwarded to our owner-managed Gmail inbox, and copied into a private Humperdinck Mail inbox. This may include sender and recipient addresses, subject, message text, original email with any attachments, and conversation status. The private archive uses restricted Cloudflare D1 and R2 storage; it is accessible to the Humperdinck owner for support, billing, privacy and legal correspondence. We do not publish customer email or expose the archive through public storage links. Messages may be retained as needed to respond to requests, manage disputes and meet legal obligations; a final automated deletion schedule for this archive has not been activated. Contact privacy@humperdinck.studio with access or deletion inquiries.

06

Accounts, payments and local voices

Google sign-in and one-time account recovery codes are available. Humperdinck stores account identifiers, selected profile information, hashed session and recovery credentials, and random installation identifiers used for online device registration. Apple login is not yet configured. Free local features may be used without registration. Paid checkout has not launched; when it does, the identified provider will process payment information and Humperdinck will retain necessary order and licensing records. Humperdinck does not offer a downloadable community library of cloned voices. Source recordings are not uploaded to such a service.

07

Your choices and requests

You control local files stored on your own computer. For access, correction or deletion requests about information held by Humperdinck, contact privacy@humperdinck.studio. The account dashboard provides recovery-code and connected-device management. If you later knowingly complete an approved account deletion, you may lose online Pro device activation and account-based license recovery. A previously downloaded perpetual offline certificate may continue to function because it cannot necessarily be remotely revoked. Minimum purchase, refund, dispute and tax records may still be retained for legally required periods, independently of the deleted login account. This is pre-launch information, not an enabled deletion service; final retention, verification and deletion procedures require review before paid sales.

08

Children and changes

Humperdinck accounts and Pro purchases are intended exclusively for adults aged 18 or older. Visitors may browse the public website without creating an account. Humperdinck does not knowingly offer account registration to minors; new account registration requires an affirmative 18+ confirmation before account creation. We record the confirmation policy version and time against the new account; we do not require a date of birth or identity documents for routine registration. An existing account may be asked to reconfirm eligibility under a separately reviewed policy. We do not intentionally solicit children's personal information through account services. If we learn that an ineligible minor has registered, we will take appropriate action consistent with applicable law, including restricting the account and addressing personal information collected. Do not upload recordings of children without the necessary rights and consents; adult uploader eligibility alone does not establish consent for another person's voice. We will update the effective date when this policy materially changes.
