Pre-launch information. This page explains current practices and planned policies. Certain commercial, account and community features are not yet available; remaining legal and operational details will be published before those features launch.
01

Who operates Humperdinck

Humperdinck is operated by Brian Larkin, a sole proprietor based in Texas, United States. For customer assistance contact support@humperdinck.studio; for privacy inquiries contact privacy@humperdinck.studio.

02

Information you choose to provide

When using local desktop features, you may enter text, select voices, upload recordings, and generate audio. Those materials are intended to be processed locally by the selected installed engine. Some features download model files or check for updates online. Required account registration, purchase and initial license-activation traffic are separate from optional desktop diagnostics, and do not require diagnostic consent. Do not assume all engines, plugins, or optional integrations operate offline; check their notices before enabling them.

03

Website and Studio

Our public pages may receive standard technical request information from our hosting provider, including IP addresses and network/security logs. The browser Studio may retrieve software or model files from Cloudflare and third-party hosting services; those providers process connection data according to their own policies. If you submit information through a feature that is not yet enabled, it is not necessarily stored.

04

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits and page performance. Cloudflare describes this tool as cookieless and not designed to fingerprint visitors. We may separately count page views and installer-link clicks by platform using anonymous event categories. These are aggregate measurements, not user profiles. Analytics availability depends on deployment settings. Desktop diagnostic reports are separate from website analytics. Humperdinck intends to offer optional diagnostics that are disabled by default and transmitted only after a user explicitly opts in. With consent, diagnostic reports may contain crash details, application and model errors, operating-system information and basic hardware or performance characteristics. The app must not automatically transmit recordings, scripts, generated audio or other voice-project content as diagnostics. Technical reports cannot be guaranteed anonymous merely because names are omitted; error messages, file paths or hardware details may sometimes identify a person, so reports should be minimized and scrubbed before transmission. Users must be able to withdraw consent for future diagnostic uploads.

05

Storage, sharing, and security

Locally saved projects, recordings and generated audio ordinarily remain under your control on your device unless you intentionally export or share them. A community upload must not occur automatically or merely because a voice was created, tested or saved; sharing requires a separate explicit publication action. Security, backups, deletion and retention on that device are your responsibility. Online services, downloads and the planned account and purchase features may involve third parties, whose applicable terms and retention periods can differ.

06

Accounts, payments and community voices

Google sign-in and one-time account recovery codes are available. Humperdinck stores account identifiers, selected profile information, hashed session and recovery credentials, and random installation identifiers used for online device registration. Apple login is not yet configured. Free local features may be used without registration. Paid checkout has not launched; when it does, the identified provider will process payment information and Humperdinck will retain necessary order and licensing records. Planned community voice uploads require explicit permission; original source recordings are not automatically uploaded.

07

Your choices and requests

You control local files stored on your own computer. For access, correction or deletion requests about information held by Humperdinck, contact privacy@humperdinck.studio. The account dashboard provides recovery-code and connected-device management. Necessary transaction and lifetime license records may be retained for legal or purchase-fulfillment reasons. Final retention, verification and deletion procedures must be established before paid sales; full self-service account deletion is not presently available.

08

Children and changes

Humperdinck accounts, Pro purchases and Community Voice Library publishing are intended exclusively for adults aged 18 or older. Visitors may browse the public website without creating an account. Humperdinck does not knowingly offer account registration or community publishing to minors; planned registration must require an affirmative age-eligibility confirmation and apply appropriate access controls. We do not intentionally solicit children's personal information through account services. If we learn that an ineligible minor has registered, we will take appropriate action consistent with applicable law, including restricting the account and addressing personal information collected. Do not upload recordings of children without the necessary rights and consents; adult uploader eligibility alone does not establish consent for another person's voice. We will update the effective date when this policy materially changes.

09

Contact information

Operator: Brian Larkin, sole proprietor, Texas, United States. Privacy: privacy@humperdinck.studio. Customer assistance: support@humperdinck.studio. A formal notice address, where required, will be provided through an appropriate channel.
