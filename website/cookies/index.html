06 / TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Cookies & Tracking.

How Humperdinck uses essential cookies, local browser storage, and website analytics.

Updated November 14, 2026

LEGAL / INFORMATION

PrivacyTermsSoftware licenseAI voiceRefundsCookiesCopyrightLicenses
01

Essential account cookies

When you sign in, Humperdinck uses secure cookies to keep your account session active and complete sign-in with Google or Apple. A short-lived cookie may also record the 18+ confirmation needed during account creation. These cookies support account access and security; they are not used for advertising.

Your account session may last up to 30 days unless you sign out or the session is revoked sooner. Temporary sign-in and age-confirmation cookies expire sooner, generally within 10 minutes.

02

Studio storage

The browser Studio may store settings and downloaded model files in your browser's storage or cache so its features work and resources do not have to be downloaded again. This storage is separate from your account sign-in cookies. Clearing it may remove local settings or require model files to be downloaded again.

03

Website analytics

Humperdinck uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general website traffic. That service is designed to operate without analytics cookies. Humperdinck also records limited first-party events, such as page visits and download interactions, to understand how the website is used. These events are not advertising cookies.

04

Managing cookies and storage

You can view, block or delete cookies and stored website data through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies can prevent account sign-in or other signed-in features from working. Clearing Studio storage may remove local preferences or cached models.

05

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if our use of cookies, browser storage or tracking technologies changes. Where the law requires consent for any new optional tracking, we will request it before using that tracking.
