Cookies & Tracking.

Pre-launch information. This page explains current practices and planned policies. Certain commercial, account and community features are not yet available; remaining legal and operational details will be published before those features launch.
01

Current analytics approach

Humperdinck plans to use Cloudflare Web Analytics and anonymous aggregate event counts. Cloudflare says its Web Analytics product does not use cookies, localStorage or fingerprinting to measure visitors. We do not describe these services as tracking users across sites.

02

Functional browser storage

The interactive Studio and model runtime may use browser memory, caches or browser storage to make the experience work, preserve settings or load model assets. These technical storage mechanisms are not the same as advertising trackers. Actual usage should be confirmed in a deployed-browser audit.

03

Hosting and security

Cloudflare and third-party services may process network information to serve pages, maintain availability and protect against abuse. Check their notices for details.

04

No unnecessary consent banner by default

We do not enable a nonessential tracking-consent banner just to display one. If optional advertising, cross-site tracking or nonessential cookies are added, we will update disclosures and implement any consent choice required by applicable law before activating them.

05

Your controls

You can manage browser storage and cookies in browser settings, use privacy protections and choose whether to use optional online features. Blocking some technical storage may make browser-based audio models less reliable.
