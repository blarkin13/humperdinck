Cookies & Tracking.

Pre-launch information. This page explains current practices and planned policies. Paid checkout and some other features remain in prelaunch testing; remaining legal and operational details will be published before those features launch.
01

Current analytics approach

Humperdinck uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and may record aggregate page or download event categories. Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as cookieless and not using localStorage or fingerprinting to measure visitors. Essential account authentication cookies are separate from this analytics service.

02

Functional browser storage

Google account sign-in uses secure, HttpOnly, same-site session and temporary OAuth flow cookies (including __Host-humperdinck_session and per-attempt __Host-humperdinck_oauth_* cookies). The browser session is valid for up to 30 days, subject to earlier revocation; OAuth attempts expire after 10 minutes. An additional secure, HttpOnly __Host-humperdinck_age18 cookie is issued for up to 10 minutes after an explicit 18+ account eligibility confirmation; it allows the server to bind that confirmation to new-account sign-in, and is not used for tracking. These are necessary for signed-in account features and do not serve advertising. The interactive Studio and model runtime may also use browser memory, caches or browser storage for settings and model assets. Actual deployed storage and third-party requests still require a browser audit.

03

Hosting and security

Cloudflare and third-party services may process network information to serve pages, maintain availability and protect against abuse. Check their notices for details.

04

No unnecessary consent banner by default

We do not enable a nonessential tracking-consent banner just to display one. If optional advertising, cross-site tracking or nonessential cookies are added, we will update disclosures and implement any consent choice required by applicable law before activating them.

05

Your controls

You can manage browser storage and cookies in browser settings, use privacy protections and choose whether to use optional online features. Blocking some technical storage may make browser-based audio models less reliable.

06

Review before publication

Before paid launch, verify hosted Studio caches and storage, current account-cookie behavior, Cloudflare analytics configuration, third-party scripts and processor retention in a real deployed browser. Document changes and update this notice if practices differ.
