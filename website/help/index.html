COMMON QUESTIONS

Using Humperdinck.

Is voice generation really local?

The desktop app runs supported speech engines on your own computer. Models may first need to be downloaded, and accounts and updates require a network connection. Rendering speed and memory requirements depend on your chosen model and hardware. Learn how local speech works.

Do Free users need an account?

No. Free desktop narration and ordinary local features are designed to work without a Humperdinck account. No monthly local narration credits or artificial length caps are planned. Hardware and model constraints still apply.

What does Pro add?

Free includes the existing studios, supported curated voices and ordinary exports. Pro is intended to unlock personal Qwen voice design, cloning with authorized recordings and saved custom Qwen voices. No community library of downloadable cloned voices is planned. Live Pro checkout is not enabled, and the final price has not been announced.

Does Humperdinck support Windows, macOS and Linux?

Native installer projects exist for Windows x64, Apple Silicon macOS and Linux x64. Not all speech engines or media formats have completed platform-specific verification. The download area shows installers when published.

Do I need a GPU?

Some smaller preset models can run on CPUs. Larger Qwen models benefit from supported GPU acceleration and may need significantly more memory. Compatibility depends on the specific engine and hardware, not just the operating system.

May I clone a celebrity or another person's voice?

Only if you have the speaker's prior express written and signed permission and all required rights. A publicly posted recording or purchased audio file does not itself grant permission. See the AI Voice & Consent Policy.

Will purchased Pro licenses work without the website?

Permanent Pro licensing is designed around an offline-verifiable signed certificate. After activation, a valid locally saved license can be restored with a compatible installer and downloaded models. Local voice projects and model files are separate from online account services.

How do I get support or report voice misuse?

For installation issues email support@humperdinck.studio. To report unauthorized voice cloning or rights concerns, contact legal@humperdinck.studio. Do not email passwords, private signing keys or unnecessary source recordings.
