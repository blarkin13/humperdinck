THE WORKFLOW

Make the most of your voice studio.

01

Create from an authorized recording

Humperdinck is developing workflows for creating voices using uploaded audio. You should only use recordings of yourself or people who have explicitly agreed to this use. Model capabilities and reliability vary.

02

Explore voice design controls

Depending on the installed engine, voices may be created or adjusted through supported presets, descriptive prompts or other voice controls. An edited voice does not necessarily reproduce identically across different models.

03

Work with saved voices

The desktop Voice Library is designed to organize custom and built-in voices separately. Confirm a saved voice with a preview before using it in a longer narration.

04

Consent is essential

Do not use the product to impersonate someone without permission or mislead listeners about a synthetic recording. Review Humperdinck's AI Voice & Consent Policy for intended safeguards and restrictions.
