01

Your content and rights

Use recordings, scripts, music, voices and other material only when you have the necessary rights or a lawful basis to use them. Creating audio with AI does not remove copyright obligations or the rights of performers and other people whose voices or likenesses may be involved.

02

Local creation, not public posting

Humperdinck is a local-first voice studio. It does not operate a community service where users post or share voice models or recordings. Your locally created voices and audio are not published by Humperdinck as a public library.

03

Report a rights concern

If you believe Humperdinck's own website, distributed software or official assets infringe your copyright or other rights, contact legal@humperdinck.studio. Include a description of the material, the rights involved, where the material appears and a way to reach you. We will review the report and take appropriate action when warranted.

04

Third-party licenses

Some fonts, models, libraries and other components are licensed by third parties. Their licenses and attributions are listed on our Third-Party Licenses page.
