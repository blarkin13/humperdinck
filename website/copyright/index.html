01

Your content and rights

You retain the rights you hold in your original content. You are responsible for obtaining all necessary copyright permissions and signed voice-consent authorizations required by Humperdinck's AI Voice & Consent Policy before using recordings, scripts, music, voices or other third-party material. AI generation does not remove copyright, performer, privacy or likeness obligations. Do not use Humperdinck to infringe others' rights or impersonate them without authorization.

02

Local creation, not public posting

Humperdinck is a local-first voice studio. It does not operate a community feature for users to upload, post, publish, distribute or share voices or recordings with other users. Humperdinck does not host a public library of user-submitted voices, and your locally created voices and audio are not automatically published.

03

Report a rights concern

If you believe material published or distributed by Humperdinck itself infringes your copyright, voice or other intellectual property rights, contact legal@humperdinck.studio. Include a description of the material, the rights involved, where the material appears and a way to reach you. We will review the report and take appropriate action when warranted.

04

Third-party licenses

Some fonts, models, libraries and other components are licensed by third parties. Their licenses and attributions are listed on our Third-Party Licenses page.
