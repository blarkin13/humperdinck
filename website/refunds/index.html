Refund & Purchase Policy.

The rules that will apply to paid Humperdinck products once sales are enabled.

Pre-launch information · Updated November 7, 2026

Pre-launch information. This page explains current practices and planned policies. Certain commercial and account features are not yet available; remaining legal and operational details will be published before those features launch.
01

Current status

Humperdinck Pro is offered through Stripe Managed Payments at $49 USD, one time, with applicable taxes displayed at checkout. This policy covers Pro purchase and activation restrictions, available customer support and legally required remedies. The account-and-computer-bound licensing change is under implementation, with previously issued licenses preserved during the transition.

02

Pricing and checkout

The purchase page must show the final price, currency, taxes where applicable, the identity of the seller or merchant of record, what features are included, supported operating systems, the requirement to register or sign in to a Humperdinck account for purchase and initial Pro activation, activation limits, and whether any recurring charge applies before an order is placed. Checkout, payment collection, tax handling, receipts, refunds and disputes are handled through the disclosed Stripe Managed Payments purchase arrangement and applicable terms.

03

One-time Pro purchases

Humperdinck Pro is a one-time lifetime software license for up to ten activated computers, with Pro updates included. Purchase and activation require signing in to the purchasing Humperdinck account on each computer. Activation is bound to that account and computer; copied licenses cannot activate another computer, and a new computer or operating-system reinstall needs another sign-in and a free activation slot. Customers can free slots in the account dashboard. Once activated, the same computer can use Pro offline without periodic checks. If the website ceases operating, already-activated computers continue offline, but new computers and reinstalled systems cannot activate. Earlier issued portable licenses continue under their prior terms until exchanged for bound licenses at the next sign-in. There are no recurring subscription fees for the Pro software license. Separately offered hosted services, models or products, if any, must be priced and disclosed independently.

04

Refunds and legally required remedies

Humperdinck Pro purchases are generally final. There is no voluntary refund or change-of-mind refund period. The account-bound, computer-bound activation requirement and ten-device limit form part of the purchase description. If you cannot activate despite meeting the published system and account requirements, contact support for assistance and applicable remedies. This does not limit refunds, cancellation rights, chargeback rights or other remedies required by applicable law, including remedies for products that are not as described or do not function as legally required. Customers must be shown this policy before payment.

05

Problems with installation or access

If the software is faulty or cannot be activated, contact support@humperdinck.studio. For billing, purchase or refund questions, contact billing@humperdinck.studio. Mandatory consumer remedies remain available regardless of the voluntary refund policy.

See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for related information.
