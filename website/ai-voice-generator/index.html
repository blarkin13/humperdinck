THE WORKFLOW

Make the most of your voice studio.

01

Create a voice that fits the story

Humperdinck brings voice design and narration into one desktop studio. Start with built-in voices, adjust available settings, or use supported creation workflows to develop a reusable voice profile. Voice design capabilities and results depend on the selected model and hardware.

02

Narrate more than a sentence

Use Narration Studio to organize longer scripts into sections and assign narrators. Review passages separately before producing a finished audio export. Some speech engines support different controls, accents and performance characteristics.

03

Local-first, not a remote rendering service

The desktop app is designed to execute supported speech engines locally after setup and model downloads. Model files may need an internet connection initially. Performance, memory usage and voice quality depend on the engine and your computer.

04

Try it before installing

The browser Studio provides a limited preview of the interface and supported capabilities. For desktop-only voice creation workflows and full project output, install Humperdinck on your computer.
