04 / RESPONSIBLE VOICE

AI Voice & Consent Policy.

Voice tools should be used with permission, transparency and respect for the people behind the recordings.

Draft for review · Updated November 7, 2026

Pre-launch legal draft. This page contains items marked for verification or completion. It is not a substitute for review by qualified counsel.
01

Consent is required

Only upload, clone, imitate, generate or distribute an identifiable person's voice when you have a lawful basis and the necessary permission, or when your use is otherwise lawful. A recording being publicly available does not by itself grant voice-cloning rights.

02

Never misrepresent identity

Do not present synthetic audio as an authentic statement, endorsement, call or performance by another person when it is not. Do not use voices to impersonate government officials, employers, relatives, financial institutions or other people for deception, harassment or fraud.

03

Prohibited harmful uses

Do not create nonconsensual intimate or exploitative voice content, facilitate scams or coercion, evade biometric voice checks, infringe rights, or knowingly spread deceptive audio in contexts where it risks significant harm.

04

Responsible publication

Clearly label synthetic content where disclosure is required or necessary to avoid misleading an audience. Obtain permission for commercial endorsement uses. Respect performer, publicity, privacy, copyright and platform rules.

05

Community voice library — not yet launched

If community voice publishing is introduced, uploads will require an explicit rights-and-consent attestation and a reporting/removal channel. This page is not evidence that moderation, verification or takedowns are currently operational.
