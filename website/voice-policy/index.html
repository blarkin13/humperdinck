04 / RESPONSIBLE VOICE

AI Voice & Consent Policy.

Voice tools should be used with permission, transparency and respect for the people behind the recordings.

Pre-launch information · Updated November 8, 2026

Pre-launch information. This page explains current practices and planned policies. Certain commercial, account and community features are not yet available; remaining legal and operational details will be published before those features launch.
01

Signed written consent is required

Do not clone, generate, imitate, recreate or distribute the recognizable voice of any real person unless you have obtained that speaker's prior, express, written consent with a handwritten or legally effective electronic signature (or the signature of a legally authorized representative). This is a contractual condition of using Humperdinck, including when creating a voice of a public figure, celebrity, neighbor, relative, coworker, or other identifiable person. You must obtain an appropriately signed authorization when recreating your own identifiable voice as well. Consent must expressly cover AI voice replication and the particular use you intend, including commercial distribution where applicable. A public interview, social-media clip, purchased recording, sample license or click-through assertion alone does not show signed speaker consent. Do not proceed if you cannot document authorization. Original fictional voices that do not imitate a real speaker and authorized built-in presets remain subject to their applicable license terms. See the Desktop Software License for the complete requirement.

02

Never misrepresent identity

Do not present synthetic audio as an authentic statement, endorsement, call or performance by another person when it is not. Do not use voices to impersonate government officials, employers, relatives, financial institutions or other people for deception, harassment or fraud.

03

Prohibited harmful uses

Do not create nonconsensual intimate or exploitative voice content, facilitate scams or coercion, evade biometric voice checks, infringe rights, or knowingly spread deceptive audio in contexts where it risks significant harm.

04

Responsible publication

Any published synthetic content must comply with applicable disclosure requirements and must not falsely imply a real person's endorsement, participation or approval. A signed voice release must include the actual use, marketing, distribution and platform permissions needed; a general permission to record a person does not automatically grant permission to create an AI replica or commercial endorsement. Respect the limits of the authorization and all performer, privacy, copyright and publicity laws. You remain responsible for every output you share.

05

Community voice library — not yet launched

Community voice publishing is planned only for registered adults aged 18 or older, and is private-by-default and opt-in for each individual voice. A publisher's age eligibility does not establish legal rights or consent to use any speaker's voice, including a minor's voice. Creating, saving or previewing a voice does not publish it. A community submission may contain only the reusable voice package, a generated voice preview sample, minimal public metadata and the rights-and-consent attestation. Original source recordings, unrelated narration scripts and project files must not be silently packaged, uploaded or published. The publication workflow must present a preview of the selected content and obtain explicit submission confirmation. A generated sample still requires appropriate speaker rights and consent. Before submitting a selected voice for publication, users must explicitly confirm they have the rights and permissions needed to share it, including consent from an identifiable speaker when required. Every new community submission will remain nonpublic and pending review until approved. The planned review includes automated checks and an approval decision; automated checks are not proof of consent or ownership, and Humperdinck may require further evidence, reject or remove a submission. By choosing to publish a voice, its creator will grant other users a non-exclusive permission to use that voice for lawful personal and commercial narration and creative projects, including monetized videos, podcasts and audiobooks. The permission covers use of the voice to generate and distribute audio outputs, not resale, redistribution, sublicensing or repackaging of the voice files, weights, embeddings or voice package itself. Publishing does not transfer ownership of any rights the creator holds or expand permissions beyond those the creator lawfully possesses. No permission is granted to falsely claim endorsement or impersonate an identifiable person. Third-party model and source-recording restrictions continue to apply. Creators can request that their published voice be removed from Humperdinck-hosted listings and files. Removal prevents new distribution by Humperdinck after processing but cannot recall copies already downloaded or shared outside Humperdinck. Removing a voice from Humperdinck stops new downloads, but does not automatically revoke licenses already granted to users who lawfully downloaded it. Those users may continue using their copies to create lawful personal and commercial narration and other projects under the original license. This continuing permission is subject to applicable law and exceptions for infringement, missing rights or consent, fraud, and other legal requirements. Humperdinck cannot guarantee deletion of copies already downloaded, and removal of a hosted listing does not validate an otherwise unlawful copy. Community voices that have been approved and published will remain subject to user reports, investigation, restriction or removal for policy or rights violations. A reporting channel, review queue, moderation/removal procedures and appeals process must be implemented before public uploads begin; this document does not claim those systems are operational.

06

Report misuse

Report suspected nonconsensual cloning, impersonation, or misuse to legal@humperdinck.studio. Users bear responsibility for consent and unauthorized voice use; see the User Defense and Indemnification and Disclaimer and Limits Regarding Misuse sections of the Desktop Software License. Local voice generation generally cannot be reviewed or prevented remotely. Community uploads are not yet open; complaint review and removal procedures must be established before publishing community voices. Publishing this policy does not mean every voice or consent document can be independently verified.
