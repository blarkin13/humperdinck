04 / RESPONSIBLE VOICE

AI Voice & Consent Policy.

Voice tools should be used with permission, transparency and respect for the people behind the recordings.

Draft for review · Updated November 7, 2026

Pre-launch legal draft. This page contains items marked for verification or completion. It is not a substitute for review by qualified counsel.
01

Consent is required

Only upload, clone, imitate, generate or distribute an identifiable person's voice when you have a lawful basis and the necessary permission, or when your use is otherwise lawful. A recording being publicly available does not by itself grant voice-cloning rights.

02

Never misrepresent identity

Do not present synthetic audio as an authentic statement, endorsement, call or performance by another person when it is not. Do not use voices to impersonate government officials, employers, relatives, financial institutions or other people for deception, harassment or fraud.

03

Prohibited harmful uses

Do not create nonconsensual intimate or exploitative voice content, facilitate scams or coercion, evade biometric voice checks, infringe rights, or knowingly spread deceptive audio in contexts where it risks significant harm.

04

Responsible publication

Clearly label synthetic content where disclosure is required or necessary to avoid misleading an audience. Obtain permission for commercial endorsement uses. Respect performer, publicity, privacy, copyright and platform rules.

05

Community voice library — not yet launched

Community voice publishing is planned as private-by-default and opt-in for each individual voice. Creating, saving or previewing a voice does not publish it. Before publishing a selected voice, users must explicitly confirm they have the rights and permissions needed to share it, including consent from an identifiable speaker when required. By choosing to publish a voice, its creator will grant other users a non-exclusive permission to use that voice for lawful personal and commercial narration and creative projects, including monetized videos, podcasts and audiobooks. The permission covers use of the voice to generate and distribute audio outputs, not resale, redistribution, sublicensing or repackaging of the voice files, weights, embeddings or voice package itself. Publishing does not transfer ownership of any rights the creator holds or expand permissions beyond those the creator lawfully possesses. No permission is granted to falsely claim endorsement or impersonate an identifiable person. Third-party model and source-recording restrictions continue to apply. Creators can request that their published voice be removed from Humperdinck-hosted listings and files. Removal prevents new distribution by Humperdinck after processing but cannot recall copies already downloaded or shared outside Humperdinck. Whether removal affects previously granted rights to use a voice in future projects must be stated explicitly in the publication agreement before launch. A reporting channel and moderation/removal process must be implemented before public uploads begin; this document does not claim those systems are operational.
