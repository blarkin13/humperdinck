THE WORKFLOW

Make the most of your voice studio.

01

What local-first means

Humperdinck is built to run supported text-to-speech engines on your own computer. Initial application, model and update downloads need connectivity, but supported local speech generation can operate without sending narration to a remote inference service.

02

Choose an engine for your computer

Different speech models have different tradeoffs in speed, memory use, voice controls and quality. Some make better sense for a laptop without a powerful GPU; others benefit from accelerated hardware.

03

Keep control of your projects

A local workflow allows you to work with scripts and generated files on your machine. Some optional account, gallery, distribution or update features require internet access, so offline operation does not apply to every feature.

04

Check your system before downloading

Humperdinck targets Windows, macOS on Apple Silicon, and Linux. Installer availability and hardware support can vary by release; check the current download information before selecting a build.
