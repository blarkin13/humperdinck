THE WORKFLOW

Make the most of your voice studio.

01

Write for the timeline

Prepare a script as manageable passages rather than a single recording. Organizing the text helps identify narration beats and potential synchronization points.

02

Select a narrator

Use built-in or supported custom voices, then listen to generated sections and adjust pacing as needed. Voice controls vary by speech engine.

03

Plan narration timing

The Video Studio workflow is designed around aligning narration and video. Available synchronization controls and supported video inputs may evolve while the desktop application is under development.

04

Export for editing

Generate audio for use in a video-editing workflow with supported output formats. Confirm the latest release's capabilities before planning a production project.
