## Use inside Humperdinck

- For **your own voice**, the app's explicit typed-name electronic signature
  records your intent to agree to the current voice policy.
- For **another speaker**, obtain this (or an equivalent appropriately scoped
  signed agreement) **before** recording or importing their voice. Humperdinck
  requires a local PDF, PNG or JPEG of the signed document and the speaker's
  name, saving it under the user's private local data folder with a SHA-256
  digest linked to the voice.
- Document submission does **not** prove that the person signed it, that they
  were legally competent to consent, that recording copyright was licensed,
  or that a particular commercial project is within scope.
- This uploader flow is distinct from **publisher-owned built-in voice packs**:
  the operator must maintain individually signed performer grants and
  original recording redistribution permissions for every shipped voice.
- Do not upload signed documents to GitHub, ChatGPT, the public website or
  ordinary telemetry; these documents may contain sensitive personal data.
- For deletion, the current voice-delete operation removes its associated
  local evidence file. Keep necessary legal copies in a controlled owner
  archive only with an actual retention basis and approved policy.

## Legal review

Confirm Texas / US UETA and ESIGN electronic signatures, cross-state rights of
publicity, state AI voice replication statutes, minors/guardians, consumer
restrictions, jurisdiction, recording copyright and performer rights before
selling or distributing a public package.

Texas UETA: https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/BC/htm/BC.322.htm
US ESIGN definition: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/7006
