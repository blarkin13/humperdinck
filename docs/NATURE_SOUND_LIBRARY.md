# Nature starter sounds & Sound Effects browser

Humperdinck's desktop Sound Effects tab includes 30 original, procedurally
synthesized starter clips. They are generated on first library access,
stored as 24 kHz mono WAV files under the user's local sound-effects folder,
and are used exactly like imported audio: preview, download, favorite,
add before/after narration, or loop as ambience in a complete render.

## What is and is not included

The included tracks are **sound designs generated by Humperdinck**, not
environmental field recordings or downloaded Pixabay content. Their realism
is limited by procedural synthesis; for production-quality natural recordings,
import audio you recorded or obtained from a source permitting your use.
The library provides an optional direct link to Pixabay's Sound Effects
section so users can find and import recordings themselves.

Pixabay's terms allow creative use of its content but prohibit redistribution
of sounds as a standalone sound library/asset pack. **Do not automatically
scrape, repackage, or redistribute Pixabay audio as starter files.**

## Browser and project workflow

- Search across titles, categories and tags. Use quick category chips,
  favorites, duration filters and sort options.
- Click the left icon to preview or stop; the progress waveform is computed
  from the actual audio energy and supports scrubbing.
- Click the heart to add favorites, download icon to save the WAV, or
  three-dot menu to rename, recategorize or delete.
- In a narration section use **Add** instead of Download; in ambience
  selection use **Select**. Existing cue volume/fade controls and the
  sound-suggestion wand are preserved.
- On first use the library creates the 30 original sounds in the desktop
  user's data folder. Installation is marked complete only afterward.
- If a user deletes a starter clip, it stays deleted: repeat app launches
  do not reinstall removed sounds. Existing imported sounds and their
  metadata are left untouched.
- The hosted public Studio shows the existing desktop-only notice; it does
  not expose private sound imports to anonymous site visitors.

## Quality and license considerations

Starter tracks are deliberately short (7–10 seconds), suitable for
experimentation, cue placement and looping with the ambience mixer.
They are **not advertised as authentic outdoor recordings** and may not
loop seamlessly without further crossfade processing.

Users may import licensed third-party recordings. Humperdinck does not grant
redistribution rights over third-party audio users import.
